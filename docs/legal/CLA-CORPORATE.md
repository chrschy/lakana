# Lakana Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Corporate Agreement"), version 3

This agreement is for a company, organisation or other legal entity ("the Company") whose
employees contribute to the Lakana project, maintained by Christopher Schymura (the
"Maintainer"). It works together with the [Individual Contributor License
Agreement](CLA.md) ("the Individual Agreement"), version 3: the Company licenses the rights
it holds in the work of its employees, and permits them to contribute. Terms defined in the
Individual Agreement have the same meaning here.

**What this is, in plain words.** The Company keeps the copyright in what its employees
contribute. It grants the Maintainer and the Maintainer's successors and assigns the same
license the Individual Agreement describes, so that an employee's Contribution can be
used even when the employer, not the employee, holds the rights. It is a license, not an
assignment.

## 1. Who signs, and how

The Company signs by sending the Maintainer a copy of this agreement, with the blanks in
section 5 filled in, signed by a person who may bind the Company, by e-mail to the
address in the privacy notice of the Individual Agreement. The Maintainer confirms receipt
in writing; the agreement applies from the confirmation. The signed copy is stored as
described in the privacy notice of the Individual Agreement.

## 2. Designated employees

The Company names the employees who may contribute under this agreement ("Designated
Employees"), by GitHub user name, in section 5. The Company may add or remove employees by
written notice to the Maintainer. A removal applies to Contributions submitted after the
Maintainer receives the notice.

Each Designated Employee also signs the Individual Agreement through the CLA status check
on their pull request, so that their own contribution is covered too. The Maintainer
treats an employee who is not named here as contributing on their own account.

## 3. Grant

For every Contribution submitted by a Designated Employee while this agreement applies,
the Company grants the Maintainer, its successors and assigns, and recipients of software
distributed by the Maintainer, the copyright license of section 2 and the patent license
of section 3 of the Individual Agreement, to the extent the Company holds the rights.
Those sections, including their two limits, apply as if the Company were "You". Sections
9 (successors and assigns) and 10 (versions) of the Individual Agreement apply too.

## 4. Representations

The Company represents that:

- the person signing may bind the Company;
- the Company is legally entitled to grant the licenses in section 3;
- each Designated Employee is permitted to make Contributions on the Company's behalf;
- each Contribution is, to the Company's knowledge, the original work of the Designated
  Employee, or is submitted under section 7 of the Individual Agreement; and
- the Company will tell the Maintainer if any of this stops being true.

Contributions are provided "AS IS", without warranties or conditions of any kind, as in
section 6 of the Individual Agreement.
