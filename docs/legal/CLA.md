## Privacy notice for signatures

**What is stored.** When You sign, the CLA bot records the GitHub user name and numeric
user ID of the account that wrote the signature comment, the time of the comment, the
number of the pull request and the identifier of the comment. The version of the Agreement
You signed is part of the file path the record is stored under. The bot does not ask for
Your e-mail address or real name. For the Corporate agreement, the company's signed copy
is stored, with the name and position of the person who signed it and the GitHub user
names of the employees it lists.

**Why.** To be able to show that You granted the license in this Agreement. The legal
basis is the legitimate interest of the Maintainer and of the project's users in being
able to prove the license (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Where.** In a repository that is not public, not in the public Lakana repository; only the
Maintainer and people the Maintainer authorises can read it. The records are stored with
GitHub, a service provider in the United States. If the project passes to a
successor under section 9, the records pass to them.

**How long.** As long as the license You granted can matter: while Your Contribution, or
a work derived from it, is distributed. The records are kept for as long as that is the case.

**Your rights.** You can ask the Maintainer which data is stored about You, ask to have
wrong data corrected, and object to the storage. A request to delete the record is
granted unless the record is still needed to prove a license for a Contribution that is
still distributed. You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

## Attribution and changes

This Agreement is adapted from the [Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor
License Agreement V2.2](https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf). Clauses 1 and 3 to 8
follow that source except for the substitutions below.

Differences from the source:

- **Entity substitution, throughout.** "The Apache Software Foundation (the
  'Foundation')" / "the Foundation" is replaced with "the Lakana project" / "the
  Maintainer" (Christopher Schymura), wherever the source names the Foundation as the
  recipient of the license grant.
- **"Work" narrowed** (clause 1, "Contribution"): the source's "any of the products
  owned or managed by the Foundation" is narrowed to "the Lakana project", since there is
  one project rather than a foundation managing many.
- **Public benefit sentence removed.** The source promises that Contributions will not be
  used in a way "contrary to the public benefit or inconsistent with its nonprofit status
  and bylaws". That promise fits a nonprofit foundation, not an individual Maintainer, and
  is too vague to apply here. It is replaced by the precise statement in section 2:
  released versions keep their Apache-2.0 license.
- **Successors and assigns, sublicensing under other terms, transfer**: sections 2, 3 and
  9. The source grants to the Foundation only, which is a long-lived organisation; the
  Maintainer is a person, so the grant also covers whoever maintains the project after
  them.
- **"Maintainer" defined** (clause 1): the definition includes the Maintainer's successors
  and assigns, which the source's "Foundation" does not need.
- **Employer clause** (clause 4): "has executed the Corporate agreement with the
  Maintainer and has named you in it" replaces the source's "has executed a separate
  Corporate CLA with the Foundation".
- **Signing mechanism replaced** (preamble): the source's mail-in process is replaced with
  the CLA status check's comment on your pull request. A company signs the Corporate
  agreement instead.
- **Section headers added** to clauses 4 to 8 for readability; the source numbers these
  clauses without headers. Sections 9 and 10 and the privacy notice have no counterpart in
  the source.
